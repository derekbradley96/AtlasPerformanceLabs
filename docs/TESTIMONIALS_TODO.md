# Getting real testimonials

## What you need (minimum 1 to re-enable the section)
- First name + last initial (e.g. "Jake M.")
- Role/context (e.g. "Online coach — 7 prep clients")  
- 2-3 sentence quote in their own words
- Written permission to publish on atlasperformancelabs.co.uk
- Optional: headshot (they can send by WhatsApp/email)

## What to ask
"Quick favour — can I use a quote from you on the Atlas site?
 Just 2-3 sentences on what it's changed for your coaching or
 training. I'll use your first name only. Reply here and I'll
 handle the rest."

## Who to ask first
Your first 3 beta coaches or clients. People who've been using
it for 4+ weeks and have messaged you about it already.

## When you have 1+ real quote
1. Add to marketingTestimonialsData.js (format is already there)
2. Remove the comment block in MarketingHomePage.jsx
3. Uncomment: <Testimonials testimonials={MARKETING_HOME_TESTIMONIALS} />

## Do NOT use placeholder names
Fake testimonials are worse than no testimonials.
Sophisticated visitors spot them immediately.
