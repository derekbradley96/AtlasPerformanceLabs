**Description (4000 chars max):**

Atlas is the coaching and performance platform built
specifically for bodybuilding athletes and the coaches
who train them.

**For coaches:**
Run your entire coaching business from one place.
Build and assign training programmes with per-set
weight and RIR targets. Set nutrition plans and
watch clients log meals in real time. Review weekly
check-ins, send feedback, and track who needs attention
today — all without switching between apps or
spreadsheets.

Competition prep coaches get peak week management,
pose check submission and review, division-specific
judging criteria, and a prep timeline showing every
athlete's show date. The features no other coaching
app has.

**For athletes:**
Know exactly what to do today. Your workout, your
macros, your habits — all in one screen. Log sets
with your coach's exact targets shown beside your
inputs. Scan any barcode to log food instantly —
and it's free. Always.

Competition athletes get their show countdown,
daily peak week protocol, posing practice timer,
and pose check submission to their coach. If you're
prepping for your first show, the built-in first
timer guide explains what to expect from peak week
to prejudging.

**What makes Atlas different:**
- Free barcode scanner — always, no paywall
- Per-set weight and RIR targets from your coach
- Food diary is coaching evidence — coach sees it live
- Competition prep built in: posing library, peak week,
  federation guides for PCA, UKBFF, NABBA, WBFF and more
- Interpreted data — not raw numbers ("you're 33g
  protein short — try chicken or a shake")
- Pillar rating system for finding the right coach
- Churn prediction alerts coaches 2-3 weeks early
- AI-drafted check-in responses save coaches hours

Start free. Personal Basic is free forever.
Coaches start with 10% commission and upgrade to Pro
or Elite as their roster grows.
